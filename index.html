Dollar General stock pitch

LIBOR Bender Trust Equity Research Project, team competition, October to December 2025

I built a forward-looking model from more than five years of 10-K data to project revenue and earnings under several operating scenarios, compared DG with five-plus peers using Porter's Five Forces and benchmarking, and used EV/EBITDA and relative multiples to show the valuation gap behind the recommendation, with the risks spelled out.

Scarletstone portfolio case

RU Valuating Scarletstone Case Competition, team competition, October to November 2025

We designed a $1M multi-asset portfolio for a client with three goals: paying for college, retiring comfortably, and saving to start a business. The portfolio held 10 positions across equities, bonds, ETFs and commodities, and we recommended a 65% stable income and 35% growth split to balance diversification, risk tolerance and expected returns.

Slides available on request.
